# AI Video Sample 1 - Product Commercial (5s)

A 5-second AI-generated product advertisement for a fictional wireless earbuds
brand, created as a visual directing sample in the style of short branded
commercial work.

**Disclosure:** this video was generated with Hailuo AI (MiniMax H3 model,
hailuoai.video). It is AI-generated content; the free-tier "MINIMAX | Hailuo AI"
watermark is retained intentionally, as required by Hailuo's Terms of Service.

## Files
- `earbuds-product-ad.mp4` - final clip (5s, 1344x768, 24fps, h264)
- `contact-sheet.png` - 3-frame contact sheet
- `prompt.txt` - the exact prompt used, verbatim
- `settings.json` - tool, model and generation settings
- `storyboard.json` - the intended beat sheet the prompt was written against

## How it was made
1. Wrote a single-shot product-ad prompt against the storyboard beats
   (macro product hero, rim lighting, suspended droplets, slow dolly-in).
2. Generated on Hailuo AI: MiniMax H3, text-to-video, 768p, 5s, 16:9
   (free tier, 35 credits).
3. Reviewed the output against the brief (subject fidelity, lighting, motion)
   and kept the first-pass result - no retries were needed for this clip.

## Reproduce
1. Create a free account on hailuoai.video (new accounts receive
   complimentary credits; 35 credits cover one 5s 768p clip).
2. Choose Video > MiniMax H3, 16:9, 768p, 5s.
3. Paste `prompt.txt` verbatim and generate.
